Learning through Monocuquear

From stories to encounters

When stories step beyond the pages of a book, extraordinary things begin to happen.

At MONOCUCOS, we believe stories can also create spaces for coming together, recognizing our differences, and discovering everything that connects us.

That is why our universe of characters and stories reaches beyond the page to meet children in schools and communities, where imagination becomes an opportunity to share, reflect, and learn to live together.

Educational experiences

Monocuqueando in schools

Some lessons take on a special meaning when we experience them together.

MONOCUCOS encounters bring children and school communities together through experiences that encourage reflection on respect, inclusion, authenticity, and living together.

Through the world of our stories, we hope every child recognizes the value of being different, discovers what they can contribute to others, and understands that everyone has a place in building a kinder community.

Our journey

Monocuqueando around the world

Cultural differences, languages, and borders do not change what brings us together.

MONOCUCOS has shared its stories and experiences with educational communities in different countries, carrying a message of inclusion, respect, and living together.

Every encounter is a chance to remember that a world for everyone begins when we recognize that we all belong to it.
